# DataCharter Trademark & Naming Policy

The DataCharter **source code** is open source under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE). The
Apache-2.0 license grants broad rights to the code — but, by its Section 6, it
grants **no rights to the project's names, logos, or brand**. This document
explains how you may (and may not) use the DataCharter name and mark. The goal
is the usual open-source one: keep the code free while preventing confusion
about what is, and isn't, official DataCharter.

## What the marks are

- The name **"DataCharter"** (and the tagline "Charter your data").
- The DataCharter logo/mark (files under `brand/`).

## You may, without asking

- **Use the software** for anything, including commercially — that's the
  Apache-2.0 grant.
- **Say your project uses or works with DataCharter** ("built on DataCharter",
  "compatible with DataCharter", "a plugin for DataCharter") — accurate,
  nominative references are fine.
- **Redistribute unmodified DataCharter** under its own name.
- Use the name in **articles, talks, tutorials, and reviews**.

## Please ask first

- Using "DataCharter" (or a confusingly similar name) as the name of **your own
  product, service, company, or a modified/forked distribution**.
- Using the DataCharter **logo** in a way that suggests official status,
  affiliation, or endorsement.
- **Merchandise** or domain names built around the mark.

Open an issue or contact the maintainers; permission is usually easy to get for
good-faith community use.

## Please don't

- Imply your fork, product, or company is **official, endorsed, or affiliated**
  when it isn't.
- Modify DataCharter and keep distributing it **under the DataCharter name** in
  a way that could mislead users about its origin — rename modified
  distributions.

## Third-party marks

DataCharter builds on other projects and respects their marks. In particular,
**DuckDB** is a trademark of the DuckDB Foundation; DataCharter is an
independent project and is **not affiliated with or endorsed by** the DuckDB
Foundation. Other names (PostgreSQL, MySQL, Snowflake, BigQuery, etc.) belong to
their respective owners and are used here only to describe compatibility.

## Status

DataCharter is not (yet) a registered trademark. This policy asserts common-law
rights in the mark and reflects the project's intent; it will be updated if a
registration is filed. This document is a community guideline, not legal advice.
